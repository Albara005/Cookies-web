Our kitchen

It all started with one family recipe

Home Crumbs is a tiny home bakery. What began as weekend baking for friends and neighbours turned into a little cookie business, run from the same kitchen table where we learned to bake.

We still do everything by hand: we brown the butter, rest the dough overnight for deeper flavour, and bake every tray ourselves. If a batch isn't perfect, it doesn't go in your box.

  • Made with loveSmall batches, hand-scooped every time.
  • Real ingredientsButter, eggs and good chocolate. Nothing artificial.
  • Baked to orderWe bake on the day you pick up.
